About Us

Discover the story behind the Bloemfontein Coffee Roasting Co. and our passion for quality coffee.

Our Story

The Bloemfontein Coffee Co. is a local coffee business based in Bloemfontein, Free State. We are passionate about providing freshly roasted coffee and quality baked products to our customers

Our goal is to create a welcoming enviroment where customers can enjoy carefully prepared coffee, freshly baked treats and friendl service.

Our Mission

To provide customers with fresh , high-quality coffee and baked products while creating a friendly and welcoming coffee-shop experience.

Our Vision

To become a trusted local coffee destination in Bloemfontein, known for quality products.

Our Values

We value quality,freshness, customer services, constistency and supporting our local community.
