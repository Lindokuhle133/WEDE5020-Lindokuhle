##Project Overview
The Bloemfontein Coffee Roasting Co. is a local coffee shop based in Bloemfontein, Free State. The website provides customers with information about the coffee shop, it's products, services, catering options and contact details.
The website was created to provide customers with an easy-to-use and responsive way to explore the business and make enquiries.
---
## Website Goals
-Provide information about the coffee shop.
-Display coffee products and baked goods.
-Provide information about catering and bulk orders.
-Allow customers to make enquiries.
-Provide contact and location information.
-Create user-friendly and responsive website.
---
##Target Audience
-Coffee lovers
-Students
-Professionals
-Local residents
-Visitors to Bloemfontein
-Business requiring catering or bulk orders
